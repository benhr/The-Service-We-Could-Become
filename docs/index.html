The Service We Could Become

The Service We Could Become

A short narrative game for PHCM9391 Health Organisations, Strategy and Change. You play the first chief executive of a new integrated care organisation in a fictional Australian region, and make decisions with partners you cannot direct.

About this prototype

This is a working prototype for the research team, not a release for students. The text is a first draft and has not been edited for credibility. Names of places and organisations are working names.

Scenes involving the Aboriginal Medical Service appear as marked placeholders. They will be written only after review by Aboriginal colleagues.

The game collects no data. Nothing you choose leaves your browser.

Episodes

Episode 1: Four partners, one inherited program

Week 1. Your first week in the job. A program you inherited runs out of money in four months, and the board wants a recommendation by Friday. About ten minutes.

Episode 3: opening only

Week 3. Only the opening is written. Paste the code from the end of Episode 1 to see how the story remembers your decisions, or start without one to see what a new player reads.

Your save code

Copy this code. Paste it at the start of the next episode and the story will remember what you decided. The code holds only your game decisions.
